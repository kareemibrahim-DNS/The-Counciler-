# Foodics Reseller Program — what we offer partners

The Reseller Program lets selected App Marketplace partners grow by using Foodics' brand, sales team and merchant network.

## Value to partners
- **Market presence:** a trusted F&B brand with 30,000+ active stores in 30+ countries.
- **Unified billing:** any deal, started by Foodics or by the partner, is invoiced and collected by Foodics. The partner focuses on closing deals.
- **Dedicated Partner Success team:** onboarding, joint sales planning, training and technical support.
- Targeted customer acquisition across verticals.
- Less operational work: Foodics handles billing and reporting.
- Recurring sales and upselling.
- Co-branding and marketing.
- Exposure through Foodics' industry events.
- Joint campaigns, webinars and qualified lead generation.

Use this when pitching the program to a new partner or explaining why a partner should route deals through Foodics.
