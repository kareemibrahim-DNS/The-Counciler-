# Requests, communication and processes

## Centralized Communication rule
- Other Foodics teams must **not** talk to reseller partners directly. Breaking this is a violation.
- One point of contact and one email channel per case.
- When Partnerships loops another team into a partner thread, it is for operational alignment only — never commercial.

## Reseller Hub (#reseller-hub on Slack)
- Where the team submits discount requests, refunds, cancellations, questions and exceptions.
- Built as Slack Workflow Builder forms, one trigger per request type (e.g. "i need discount", "i need refund", "i need cancellation"). No upgrade/downgrade forms.
- **Kareem approves every request himself.** He involves Hatem only when he chooses. Kareem invites channel members himself.

## MP block requests
- Tickets come only from Slack channel **#mp-block-requests**.
- Accounts blocked by the MP block team go to the reseller partner to deactivate the service — sent by Slack and email.
- Partner deadline: **15 days**. Deactivation only — no Salesforce churn flag.

## Partner onboarding (being built in Orbit)
1. Add the partner's email in Orbit → it sends the reseller contract (Word) plus an onboarding form.
2. Form collects: CR (mandatory), tax card (optional), cost-price declaration, line items with per-country or fixed-USD cost prices, head-and-tail vs static model, flexible vs one-way pricing, billing frequency, country tax document, VAT treatment, bank details.
3. Submissions are reviewed in Orbit.
4. The reviewed submission goes to **Ozlem**, who starts configuration.
- An Onboarding SOP is still to be written to match the Offboarding SOP.

## Partner offboarding
- Follows **SOP-OPS-OFFB-001 v1.4**.

## Orbit (Kareem's partner management app)
- Holds partners, tasks, cases, ARR, targets, policies, line items and MP blocks.
- No one is pre-approved: each person requests access and Kareem grants what they need. Sales and Account Management use it as Requesters.
- When Slack and Salesforce data conflict, Kareem decides.
