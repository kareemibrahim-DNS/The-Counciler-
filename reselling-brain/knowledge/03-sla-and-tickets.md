# SLAs and ticket statuses

SLAs are the maximum. Earlier is better. Working days are KSA working days (Sunday–Thursday).

## Case SLAs (Orbit)
| SLA | Case types |
| --- | --- |
| 48 working hours | Cancellation, renewal, deactivation, missing IDs, collection, partner support, 1:1 email |
| 96 working hours | Refund, billing adjustment, settlement, payment request, credit note, email needing attachment review |
| 15 days | MP block deactivation by the partner (changed from 10 days in Nov 2026) |
| 12 hours | Urgent — Critical |
| 24 hours | Urgent — High |

## Email SLAs
- **1:1 email:** respond within 48 working hours. Reply if they are waiting on us; follow up if we are waiting on them.
- **Email needing attachment check/review:** respond within 96 working hours. Follow up on the 4th day if we are waiting on them.

## Team response SLA (pinned in Slack)
- Inbound email before 12 PM → same day; otherwise +1 working day.
- Outbound MoM / documentation → +1 working day.
- Slack before 4 PM → same day; after 4 PM → +1 working day.

## Requests from other Foodics teams to Partnerships
- Usual cases: 48 working hours for response, resolution or feedback.
- Urgent cases: 24 working hours.

## Partner ticket statuses
- **Open:** newly received. Must move to another status within 24 hours.
- **Due:** partner asked and Foodics has not answered, or Foodics asked and the partner has not answered.
- **Pending:** the initiator got a reply with a question and has not replied back; or not solved with a clear reason.
- **Urgent:** someone asked for a requirement with no response; or a merchant has an issue and nobody from any side answers or follows up.
- **Closed:** our side replies last with "thank you", "done" or "case closed".
- A closed thread that someone re-opens goes back to Open with the same SLA and flow.
- All tickets are handled within 24 hours in the normal flow, during working hours Sunday–Thursday (hours as recorded: "8:00 to 18:00" — confirm with Kareem).
