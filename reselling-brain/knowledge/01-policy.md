# Financial Actions & Compensation Policy (22 Sep 2026)

Scope: KSA, Kuwait, UAE, Jordan, Egypt. Every partnership-team request is validated against this policy.

## Downgrade, upgrade, refund (partial or total), cancellation
- Allowed only within **30 days of the invoice date** — never counted from the service start date.
- Request by email, stating merchant, action and reason.
- Note: the Reseller Hub request forms offer refund and cancellation only (no upgrade/downgrade request types).

## Purchased but not live
- A service bought but not yet live gives **no refund right**.
- Remedy: a **billing adjustment** (shift the start/end date).
- A billing adjustment never substitutes for a refund, discount or cash.

## Compensation
- Needs written, recorded or screenshot evidence. Verbal-only claims are not accepted.
- Never cash. Granted as **extended duration on the same service**.

## Discounts
- New acquisitions only. Valid **one year**. Not on renewal. No carry-over to expansions.
- The request must include: F5, brand, branch count, discount %, scope (one or several F5), expansion plan.

## Renewals
- Account owners must flag a non-renewal to the partnership team **before the renewal date**. The Salesforce alert is only a signal, not the flag.

## Markets and currency
- Deals outside the five markets are sold in **USD**, including expansions of existing brands.

## Escalation and outreach
- Out-of-profile partners or merchants go to the Head of Partnerships or Marketplace Ops.
- New partner outreach needs approval from Kareem or Hatem.

## Partner-side deactivation
- Does not change the merchant's financial standing with Foodics and does not trigger a refund.

## Standard T&C block (add to every deal or discount email reply)
1. On renewal the merchant goes back to list prices — discounts are not recurring.
2. Additional branches are not included in the deal.
3. The 30-day refund window is calculated from the issuance date.
4. The subscription counts from the issuance date.
